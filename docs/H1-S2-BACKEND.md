## Decisions (Claude, under the owner's 2026-10-10 delegation — reversible)

- **Schema follows S1, not the spec's draft:** provider identities live in `identities` (one user, many providers),
  so `users` gains only `version_seq`. H1 §5.2 is amended to match.
- **The client moves to its own origin at S4** (Cloudflare Pages, free, `*.pages.dev` — on the public suffix list, so
  no other site shares its storage). `komogortev.github.io` hosts every Pages site the owner has, and any of them could
  read a bearer token kept there (S1 session-2 review). This amends H1 §12 Q8's "stay on `github.io`"; its point (no
  custom domain, bearer tokens over cookies) stands. Until S4 the token reads only `/me`.
- **Worker tests live in `api/test/` under `api`'s own vitest 4**; the app's `vitest --dir src` (v2) never sees them.

### Protocol amendments made by step 4 (H1 §6.1 follows)

- Push status gains **`retry`**: still pending after the last round — keep the outbox row and push again.
- A mutation needs `entityId` equal to its payload `id` (case-folded), `op` of `upsert`/`delete`, `baseVersion` null or an integer ≥ 1, and for `delete` a payload with `deletedAt`. Anything else is `rejected` for that mutation only; the request still answers 200 in order.
- Request bounds: ≤ 100 mutations (400) and ≤ 1,000,000 bytes (413). The client splits a flush by size as well as count.
- **Client contract:** coalesce outbox rows per entity before pushing (payloads are full entries, so the latest wins; base = the earliest row's base). Two rows for one entity in one request work, but the second one is flagged as a conflict and leaves a revision.
- `GET /entries?from&to&q&deleted=1&limit&cursor`: newest first, UTC days on `createdAt` (both ends inclusive), `q` a case-insensitive (ASCII) substring of the body, 1–100 characters, ≤ 200 per page, `next` is an opaque cursor.

### Known limits

- **Replay after another device's edit.** A push whose response was lost, replayed after a second device edited the same entry, applies the old content over the newer one (the newer text is kept in `entry_revisions`, flagged `conflict`). Exact replay detection would need a mutation id; with one owner and ≤ 500 requests a day the window is small and nothing is lost. Re-entry: before a second active writer, or when a conflict count in logs is not near zero.
- **Un-delete.** None in H1 (a deleted entry stays deleted on any push). An explicit restore would need its own op.
- **D1 Time Travel vs pull cursors.** `version_seq` is per account; after a point-in-time restore it goes back, and a device whose cursor is ahead pulls nothing until the counter catches up (no reset signal). Re-entry: before the first restore drill, or in S3 when the client `sync/` loop is built — it should reset its cursor when a pull returns a cursor lower than its own.
- **CPU at the largest push.** 100 entries near the 1 MB bound are cleaned (NFC) and re-serialised in one request; the S1 CPU figures were for tiny requests. Step 7 measures it; the free plan allows 10 ms.
- **D1 free plan: 50 queries per invocation.** A push is 2 queries per round plus auth; whether one `batch()` counts as one query on the free plan is not confirmed by the limits page. Step 7 measures a 100-mutation push on the deployed Worker.
